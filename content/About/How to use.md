## On the website

- **Start from a challenge** — open the [[Challenge map]]: each challenge lists the metrics that inform it, how they are calculated, which tools can do it, and which student projects did.
- **Start from a metric** — every metric page says *what it measures*, *how it is calculated* (formula or tool), which tools can calculate it and which case studies used it. The [[Metric catalogue]] lists them all.
- **Start from a tool** — every tool page lists the metrics it can calculate and the case studies that used it. See the [[Tool directory]].
- **Search** — ignores accents (*zizkova* finds *Žižková*), matches parts of words (*bug* finds *Ladybug*) and tolerates small typos (*stromwater*).
- **Tags** — click a tag such as `category/energy` or `platform/rhino-grasshopper` to list everything in that group.
