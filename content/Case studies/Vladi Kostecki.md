## Project

The site is a former three-building industrial plant on a Prague 9 brownfield, wedged between growing housing and a green corridor, where a conventional big-box warehouse would cut neighbourhoods off. The project keeps the existing structure and reorganises it into zones: logistics and loading docks with storage in the south-east, retained manufacturing in the north and a two-storey public commercial wing on the west carried by an expressive exoskeleton beam system and adaptive vertical façade panels. The adjoining brownfield becomes a 34,000 m² public park divided into four landscape zones from managed planting to dense habitat, and a pedestrian underpass separates residents from truck traffic. KPIs cover embodied and operational carbon (including carbon per pallet), monthly energy use before and after envelope renovation, pedestrian–truck intersections and green space.

## How the student framed the challenge

- Adaptive reuse to cut embodied carbon
- Urban integration of logistics buildings
- Truck and pedestrian conflict on logistics sites
- Brownfield conversion into public green space

## Trade-offs

- More green space on the property means less storage space (green space area vs. storage capacity) (questionnaire).
- Reuse with an upgraded envelope and adaptive façade raises upfront cost and embodied carbon of the renovation but lowers operational energy and running costs (CapEx/embodied carbon vs. operational energy) (questionnaire 4.4).

## Decision

Retain and reconstruct the existing industrial structure rather than build new, supported by the annualised carbon comparison (approx. 232 vs. 745 tCO2e/year). Upgrade the envelope with insulation and sun-responsive façade panels (approx. 37.5% less energy), add a pedestrian underpass to remove all pedestrian–truck crossings, and turn the brownfield into a zoned public park.

## Open questions

- The design does not yet account for future expansion if more land is purchased (questionnaire 1.2–1.3).
- No grey-water strategy yet; biodiversity measures are general (questionnaire).
- Questionnaire KPIs (truck turnaround time, queue time at bays, worker safety incidents/TRIR, dead space, truck flow-through rate) are not quantified in the portfolio.
- Concept clarity and testing of alternatives named as biggest gaps; alternatives for the green space vs. storage trade-off not yet developed (questionnaire 6.2).
