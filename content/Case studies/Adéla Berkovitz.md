## Project

As EV use grows, demand for battery recycling is rising, but recycling plants such as the case studies REDUX (Bremerhaven) and Li-Cycle Spoke (Sülzetal) are process-efficient yet isolated, generic and poorly integrated with landscape and neighbours. The project remodels the existing Pragometal metal and battery recycling site in Česká Lípa, on an industrial–residential edge with asphalt-dominated yards, into a facility dedicated to battery waste within the original site boundaries. The single big box is split into three volumes: a main processing hall stepped in height for large machinery, an admin office building with sheltered nooks and a roof terrace, and a separate hazardous-waste storage building. The hazardous building is placed furthest from the office and next to the road for fire-brigade access. Staff, trucks and fire services get separate site entrances. Landscape moves include green roofs on the processing and office buildings, a climbing-plant trellis façade, a communal green zone, a 50 cm earth berm with shrubs facing roads and housing, perimeter bio-corridors, and a rainwater pond that also serves as a fire-water reserve.

## Trade-offs

- Splitting the mass into separate buildings is less financially efficient (cost) but gives human scale, better neighbourhood integration and passage for small animals (biodiversity / visual impact).
- A linear logistics flow (processing efficiency, less forklift travel) blocks a bio-corridor through the plot, so ecological elements move to the perimeter (questionnaire).
- Green roof coverage conflicts with hazard safety: the hazardous storage building cannot have a green roof.
- Rooftop car parking would save land but needs heavier columns for the larger loads (structural material/embodied carbon), so parking stays on the ground (consultation).

## Decision

Hazard and fire-safety needs plus neighbourhood integration led to three separate volumes, with hazardous storage placed furthest from the office and next to fire-brigade access. Separate entrances were given to staff (north), trucks and fire services (east). A berm, green roofs and façades, and a rainwater pond doubling as fire-water reserve soften the facility's impact.

## Open questions

- A second alternative at the opposite end of the trade-off was not yet developed (questionnaire).
- At mid-term the student wanted to move to a more expandable greenfield or forest-edge plot. The final portfolio stays on the original Česká Lípa site.
