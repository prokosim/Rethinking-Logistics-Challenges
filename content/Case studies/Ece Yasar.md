## Project

The project takes an existing small cross-dock facility in Kitzingen that sits between two forest patches separated by two major roads, leaving wildlife movement blocked at ground level and the warehouse roof as unused concrete. Instead of demolition, the roof is reinterpreted as an ecological platform: a planted bridge rises gradually from one forest edge, crosses the roads at roof height (7.0 m), continues as a native green roof over about half the roof area, and descends to the forest on the other side. The existing PV panels are retained alongside the green roof, and the bridge also provides shaded space for employees and a public landscape route for the nearby residential neighbourhood. An LCA of the existing building (One Click LCA, A1–A5) was used to show that concrete, HVAC and plastic membranes dominate the footprint.

## How the student framed the challenge

- Habitat fragmentation by logistics roads
- Unused flat warehouse roofs
- Stormwater runoff from impermeable roofs
- Hard edge between logistics and community

## Tools

- [[One Click LCA]] — LCA of the existing Walther cross-dock (A1–A5, EN 15804) with global warming bubble charts by resource type, plus the Biodiversity Stress tool for supply-chain ecological impact

## Trade-offs

- Improved truck flow and dock capacity (logistics efficiency) reduces permeable area (stormwater), which the student proposes to mitigate with added green infrastructure (questionnaire).
- Material biodiversity stress from the building (7,920 PDF/m²/yr) is set against on-site habitat creation, but the offset is only claimed qualitatively.

## Decision

The KPI comparison (0% green roof, no wildlife connection, all roof runoff to drainage) supported turning the existing roof into a planted platform and extending it as a green bridge across the roads to reconnect the two forest patches, while keeping the existing PV and the building itself (no demolition).

## Open questions

- Most KPIs are reported qualitatively; only green roof coverage, existing vegetated share and LCA biodiversity stress have numbers.
- Stormwater reduction, carbon sequestration and energy load of the proposal are not quantified.
- Electricity price for the location and CapEx/OpEx split were not known (questionnaire).
- Student reports needing a deeper understanding of the KPI evidence base, and did not account for cost of transporting materials / sourcing local materials (questionnaire 4.4, 6.2).
