## What it does

Free interactive benchmarking dashboard from Ramboll that normalises building carbon assessments made with different methods so projects can be compared against peers. The September 2024 update covers 203 assessments across 7 countries and 11 building typologies.

## What it can calculate

- Whole-life carbon benchmarks of building LCAs (kgCO2e/m²) by typology and life-cycle scope
- Concrete Usage Index (concrete volume per m²)
- Comparison across 12 life-cycle scope combinations incl. modules A, B, C and operational B6 where available

## Status

Launched March 2024 (Buildings and Climate Global Forum); expanded Sept 2024. Benchmark database, not a project calculator.
