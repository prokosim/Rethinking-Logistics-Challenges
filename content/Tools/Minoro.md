## What it does

Free web platform that curates guidance, policies, incentives and a stepwise process for managing whole-life carbon in buildings and infrastructure, organised by stakeholder role and region. It includes a downloadable carbon-management toolkit but does not run calculations itself.

## What it can calculate

Nothing directly — it is a knowledge resource that supports decisions rather than a calculator.

Active (2026), 'regularly updated'; content is proprietary IP of Grimshaw (free to use).
