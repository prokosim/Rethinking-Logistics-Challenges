## What it does

Grasshopper plugin from ETH Zurich for real-time simplified whole-building LCA in early design. It links geometry to Swiss KBOB material data and reports embodied GWP, primary energy and eco-points, either top-down at building level or bottom-up at component level.

## What it can calculate

- Global Warming Potential (kgCO2eq)
- Non-renewable primary energy (PENRE)
- Swiss eco-points (UBP)
- Embodied impacts at building, element and component level (Swiss KBOB database)
