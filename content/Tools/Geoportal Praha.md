## What it does

Prague's official map and open-data portal: land use, utility networks, noise maps, transport and many other layers. Students use it as the evidence base for site analysis in Prague.

## What it can calculate

- Site data layers (land use, utilities, noise, transport) for analysis

## Status

Data source rather than a calculator.
