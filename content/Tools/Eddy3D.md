## What it does

Grasshopper plugin for outdoor wind and microclimate simulation with OpenFOAM. It sets up urban CFD cases and combines wind results with weather data for outdoor comfort studies.

## What it can calculate

- Pedestrian-level wind speed and wind factors
- Wind comfort maps
- Inputs for outdoor thermal comfort (UTCI)
