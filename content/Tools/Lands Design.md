## What it does

BIM landscape-design plugin for Rhino and AutoCAD with a database of 8,000+ plant species. It covers planting, terrain modelling, irrigation and quantity schedules, and can simulate plant growth and seasonal change for visualisation.

## What it can calculate

- Plant growth simulation over years
- Seasonal appearance of vegetation
- Earthwork cut-and-fill volumes
- Irrigation network design (pipe lengths, sprinkler counts, schedules)
- Automatic plant/element quantity take-offs exported to Excel
