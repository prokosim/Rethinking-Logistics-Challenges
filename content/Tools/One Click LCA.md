## What it does

Web-based construction LCA platform with what it calls the world's largest construction LCA database (500,000+ datasets). It calculates embodied and whole-life carbon from BIM/CAD quantities and produces compliance reports for green-building certifications and national regulations.

## What it can calculate

- Whole-building life-cycle assessment (EN 15978 modules A–D)
- Global Warming Potential A1–A3 and full life cycle (kgCO2e)
- Other LCA impact categories
- Life-cycle costing (LCC)
- EPD generation for manufacturers
- Credits/documentation for LEED, BREEAM, DGNB and 140+ standards

## Status

Active (2026). Paid tiers; free 'Planetary' access for education.
