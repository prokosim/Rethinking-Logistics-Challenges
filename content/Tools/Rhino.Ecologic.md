## What it does

Ecological simulation framework for Rhino/Grasshopper that voxelises landscapes, urban sites or building envelopes and simulates how plant communities grow, compete and change over time. Designers get biomass, species distribution and biodiversity as measurable outcomes alongside soil and light maps.

## What it can calculate

- Spatio-temporal plant species distribution maps (3D, voxel-based)
- Biomass accumulation per species over time
- Biodiversity / species richness indices
- Plant abundance and voxel occupation
- Soil condition maps (type, depth, water retention capacity)
- Light availability maps from geometric shading
- Plant community succession, competition, dispersal and mortality (Joschinski model)
