## What it does

GPU-accelerated ray-tracing analysis plugin for Grasshopper from Foster + Partners, meant for fast solar and daylight studies on large, complex urban models. Foster + Partners claim it runs sunlight-hour analysis up to 10,000x faster and radiation analysis up to 800x faster than conventional CPU tools.

## What it can calculate

- Solar radiation / irradiance
- Daylight (sky illumination)
- Sunlight hours
- Sunlight obstruction
- Shading mask

## Status

Launched publicly 2025-04-29; free; still beta with a 90-day build expiry (must update). Identified as the Foster + Partners Cyclops; there is no other Grasshopper solar 'Cyclops' plugin.
