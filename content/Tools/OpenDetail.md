## What it does

Crowd-sourced, peer-reviewed open database of architectural construction details using bio-based materials (timber, hemp, clay, reed, mycelium). Users share and download details to help low-carbon bio-based construction spread. It is a knowledge repository, not a calculator.

## What it can calculate

Nothing directly — it is a knowledge resource that supports decisions rather than a calculator.

## Status

Developed 2023–2024, publicly launched/reported Feb 2026 (ArchDaily 2026-02-17). Whether details carry carbon (kgCO2e) data, and the content license, were not verified.
