## What it does

Physics engine for Grasshopper used for form-finding: hanging-chain (catenary) arches and shells, tensile structures and relaxation. It shapes efficient structural forms; it does not check stresses or code compliance.

## What it can calculate

- Form-found geometry under loads (catenaries, funicular shells)

## Status

Kangaroo2 ships with Rhino 7 and 8.
