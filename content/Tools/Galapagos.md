## What it does

Evolutionary (genetic-algorithm) solver built into Grasshopper. It searches parameter combinations to minimise or maximise a fitness value — for example façade perimeter or cost at a fixed floor area.

## What it can calculate

- Optimised parameter sets for a single objective
