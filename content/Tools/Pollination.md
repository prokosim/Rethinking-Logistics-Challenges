## What it does

Commercial companion to the open-source Ladybug Tools, made by the same company. Its Rhino and Revit plugins build, clean and validate simulation-ready energy and daylight models from CAD/BIM geometry and export them to many BEM formats; a cloud platform runs Ladybug Tools simulation recipes at scale. The Grasshopper plugin is free; Rhino plugin about USD 1,375/yr and Revit plugin about USD 2,750/yr (2026 pricing page), with 90% student discount.

## What it can calculate

- Model preparation/translation of energy & Radiance models (exports HBJSON, DFJSON, OSM, IDF, gbXML, IES VE GEM, eQuest INP, IDA ICE IDM, CBECC SDDXML)
- Energy simulation (via Honeybee/OpenStudio/EnergyPlus engines)
- Daylight simulation (via Radiance engines)
- Thermal comfort simulation
- Cloud execution of Ladybug Tools simulation recipes
