## What it does

Visual programming environment built into Rhino. It is the host for most tools in this knowledge base (Ladybug Tools, Karamba3D, Eddy3D, Kangaroo, Galapagos …) and, with custom scripts, can itself calculate geometric metrics such as areas, distances, coverage ratios and buffers.

## What it can calculate

- Areas, lengths, distances and ratios from geometry (custom scripts)
- Parametric variants for comparison

## Status

Part of Rhino 6–8.
