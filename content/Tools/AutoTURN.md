## What it does

Vehicle swept-path analysis software. It simulates the turning manoeuvres of trucks and other vehicles to check and size access roads, yards, aprons and loading bays.

## What it can calculate

- Vehicle swept paths and turning envelopes
- Required apron depth and bay dimensions
