## Challenge

Data centres are the newest logistics typology: they store data instead of goods, and turn almost all their electricity into heat. Cooling them takes energy and water; their waste heat can warm their neighbours.

## Design questions

- How much heat does the facility produce, and who could use it?
- How efficient is the cooling (PUE, water use)?
- Can climate (wind, cold air) cool the building passively?
