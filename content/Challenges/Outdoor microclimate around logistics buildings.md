## Challenge

Large buildings and paved yards change the microclimate around them: wind corridors, heat islands, missing shade and noise. Outdoor comfort decides whether public spaces around logistics will be used.

## Design questions

- Are outdoor spaces sheltered from strong wind?
- Do they get sun in winter and shade in summer?
- How hot do roofs and yards get?
