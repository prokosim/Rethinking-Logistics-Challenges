## Challenge

Roofs, yards and parking make logistics sites almost fully sealed. Rainwater runs off quickly, overloading drainage and missing the chance to cool, irrigate and recharge groundwater.

## Design questions

- How much of the site is sealed?
- How much rainwater can be retained and reused on site?
- How much does runoff decrease compared with today?
