## Challenge

Big boxes, blank façades and night-time activity change the daily view and life of neighbours. Acceptance depends on scale, screening, noise and what the project offers back.

## Design questions

- How much of the neighbours' view is obstructed?
- How do residents perceive the logistics activity?
- Can greenery or façade design reduce the visual impact?
