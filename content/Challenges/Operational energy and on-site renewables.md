## Challenge

Large volumes, heated or cooled storage and automation make logistics energy-intensive — but huge roofs also make them potential power plants.

## Design questions

- Is the building heating- or cooling-dominated, and what drives it?
- How much of the demand can rooftop PV cover?
- How do energy savings compare with extra investment over 30 years?
