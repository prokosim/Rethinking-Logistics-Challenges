## Challenge

Truck yards, aprons and docks take a large share of logistics sites. Their layout decides throughput, waiting times, safety and how much land is paved.

## Design questions

- How many trucks per hour must the site handle?
- How many docks are really needed?
- How much yard area does each dock require?
